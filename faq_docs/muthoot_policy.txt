Muthoot FinCorp Gold Loan Policy & General Information

# Gold Loan Eligibility
- Age: Any Indian citizen between 18 and 80 years of age is eligible.
- Gold Requirement: You can pledge gold ornaments with a purity between 18 and 22 Karat.
- Employment: The loan is available to various profiles, including homemakers, farmers, working professionals, and business owners.

# Documentation & KYC
KYC verification is mandatory before loan approval. You will need valid KYC documents, which include:
- Proof of Identity: Aadhaar card, PAN card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License.
- Proof of Address: Aadhaar card, Passport, Voter ID, Utility Bills, etc.

# Interest Rates & Fees
- Interest rates start from as low as 9.95% p.a.
- Interest rates are not fixed for everyone; they depend on the specific loan scheme chosen, the loan-to-value (LTV) ratio, the purity and weight of the pledged gold, and the loan tenure.
- Processing fees apply and vary depending on the scheme (can be as low as Rs 20 plus applicable taxes).

# Loan Details & Tenure
- Maximum loan tenure is generally up to 12 months.
- Loan Amount: You can typically get a loan amount of up to 75% of the value of the gold pledged, subject to branch appraisal.
- End Use: There are no restrictions on the end use of the funds. They can be used for weddings, medical emergencies, education, business needs, or any personal requirement.
- Speed: Processing is extremely quick, often allowing customers to walk into a branch and receive the loan in a single visit.

# Repayment Options
- Customers can repay their loans through UPI, Net Banking, Cash at Branch, and online payment portals.
- Flexible repayment options are available, including monthly interest payments and other schemes suited to different financial needs.

# Other Products
In addition to Gold Loans, Muthoot FinCorp offers Small Business Loans, Loan against Property (LAP), Secured & Unsecured Business Loans, Affordable Housing Loans, Two-Wheeler Loans, Used Car Loans, Domestic & International Money Transfer, Foreign Exchange, Insurance Products & Services, Wealth Management Services, and Affordable Gold Jewellery.
