GOLD LOAN ============================================================

Question: What is a gold loan? Question: Explain gold loan. Question:
Tell me about gold loan. Question: Loan against gold. Question: Gold

Question: Need money using gold. Question: Can I keep my ornaments as

ornaments are pledged as collateral. Loan eligibility depends on gold
purity, valuation, applicable regulations and company policies.

Question: How much loan can I get? Answer: Customers may receive up to
75% of the assessed value of eligible pledged gold, subject to RBI
regulations and internal policies.

Question: What gold purity is accepted? Answer: Generally eligible gold
ornaments between 18 and 22 karats are accepted after branch
verification.

Question: Can I pledge gold coins? Answer: Acceptance depends on company
policy and applicable regulations. Please contact the nearest branch.

Question: Can I pledge gold bars? Answer: Gold bars are generally not
accepted under standard gold loan products unless specifically permitted
by company policy.
